Digital Magic 8 Ball

About This Project

A Halloween costume prop. The shell is a 100 mm 3D printed sphere, with a round GC9A01 behind a 30 mm crystal. A Seeed XIAO ESP32-C6 and a LIS2DH12 accelerometer sit inside. Shake the sphere and it shows an answer.

There is no Wi-Fi, no Bluetooth, no app, and no speaker. A recessed latching button is the power switch. The ball holds 221 answers, including the 20 classic lines.

The pictures are the round display you see through the crystal.

Shell
100 mm 3D printed sphere
Display
GC9A01, 240 by 240, behind a 30 mm crystal
Board

Motion
